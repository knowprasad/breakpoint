# Product Specification: `breakpoint`
**The Battle-Tested Engineering Library & Production Disaster Encyclopedia**

## 1. Vision & Core Value Proposition
Developer education overwhelmingly focuses on the "happy path"—clean code on localhost with infinite bandwidth and single-user loads. In real production, senior engineering competence is defined by **failure imagination**: understanding the exact boundary conditions, concurrency race conditions, memory leaks, and cascading retry storms that bring down billion-dollar platforms.

`breakpoint` provides:
1. **The Production Disaster Encyclopedia:** Deep forensic post-mortems of history's most instructive outages (CrowdStrike, Cloudflare, GitLab, Shopify, AWS S3, Meta, GitHub, Stripe, Knight Capital).
2. **The Timeless Technical Library:** Canonical books (DDIA, OSTEP, Google SRE), iconic talks (Rich Hickey, Bryan Cantrill, antirez), and seminal papers (Raft, Lamport clocks).
3. **Bidirectional Knowledge Graph:** Seamless two-way navigation connecting real-world failures to the foundational computer science mechanisms that mitigate them.
4. **Interactive Failure Simulator:** Hands-on diagnostic drills simulating live production crises.
5. **Bi-Weekly Ambient Digest:** Wednesday Systems Autopsies & Sunday Architecture Deep-Dives.

## 2. Target Audience & Jobs-to-be-Done
* **Senior Software Engineers & Architects:** Deepen operational intuition, audit production architectures for hidden failure modes, and review real post-mortem mitigations.
* **Staff+ Engineers & Tech Leads:** Prepare checklists for architectural design reviews and PR review guardrails.
* **System Design Candidates:** Master real-world failure patterns beyond simplistic textbook designs.
* **SRE & Production Operations:** Learn battle-tested recovery patterns, canary strategies, and defensive monitoring.
